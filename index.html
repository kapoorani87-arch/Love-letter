💌 My Letter For You

Meri Ekampreet 🧿🫂🫀


Aapke saath bitaya hua har ek second mere liye bahut khaas hai. Aap meri life ka woh beautiful part ho jise main kabhi khona nahi chahta.


Aap bahut zyada sundar ho. Aap bilkul swarg se utri hui apsara, ek hoor pari ho. 🥹❤️


Main bahut zyada lucky hoon ki aap meri life mein ho. Aapke saath baat karna, hasna aur chhoti-chhoti baatein share karna mere liye bahut special hai.


Aur haan, agar maine kabhi aapko hurt kiya ho ya gusse mein aapse loud voice mein baat ki ho toh I'm really sorry. ❤️ Mera intention kabhi bhi aapko hurt karna nahi hota.


Main aapko gussa bhi dilata hoon (aur shayad dilata rahunga 😂), lekin sach bataun toh aap gusse mein bhi bahut cute lagti ho. 😭❤️


Aap hamesha khush raha karo. Aapki khushi mein meri khushi hai. 🫂


Maine aaj tak itni zyada sundar ladki nahi dekhi. Aapki smile hi alag level ki hai. ❤️


Bas meri ek wish hai ki hum hamesha aise hi saath rahein, saath mein hasein, ek doosre ko support karein aur bahut saari beautiful memories banayein.


Aur ek vaada aapse karta hoon — main apni life mein successful person banne ke liye poori mehnat karunga. Main chahta hoon ki ek din main itna capable banu ki aapke saath shaadi karne ka apna sapna poora kar sakun. ❤️ Main aapko hamesha khush rakhne aur aapka saath dene ki poori koshish karunga. 🫂🧿


Aur haan... future mein do chhote-chhote bacche bhi ho 😂❤️ Bas kisi ki nazar na lage. 🧿


Always yours,
Anirudh ❤️
